# Attack plan: expanding the research

2026-11-07. A running plan for widening the market research. Each item gets a
checkbox; the commit history shows when it landed. **Wave 1 is done** (streams
01–06, `ideas.md`, the drafts, the ideas page, and the web-design research plus
sketches).

## The gaps wave 1 left

1. **Money.** No real pricing model or unit economics. Offering-model ticket 05
   (pricing basis) is still open, and the drafts are full of `$[__]`.
2. **Competitors.** Named, but never taken apart: what they sell, at what price,
   how they're reviewed, and where they're weak.
3. **Running the business.** Suppliers, equipment, software, insurance,
   licences, contracts and taxes. A new studio needs all of it, and none of it
   is mapped.
4. **Business-to-business buyers.** Corporate, hotel, real estate, retail and
   civic buyers on the coast came up only in passing.
5. **Skill and credibility.** Certifications, education, conventions and the
   balloon-artist community, plus how artists actually grow on social media.

## Wave 2: research (six streams, in parallel)

Files go in `research/`, cited, with confidence per claim, like wave 1.

- [x] **07 Pricing and unit economics.** Published prices nationally and in the
      Pacific Northwest, by product (per-foot garland, arches, sculpture,
      proposals, rental surprise setups, subscriptions, the tent). Material
      cost per foot, labour hours, margins, deposits and minimums. → Input to
      offering-model ticket 05.
- [x] **08 Competitor teardown.** Salem Balloons and Flowers, Bouquets &
      Balloons, Pop & Go, Balloon Artisan, Portland Balloon Decor, Balloon
      Theories, plus coast rental and decor companies. Offerings, prices,
      service area, reviews, positioning and gaps.
- [x] **09 Operations: suppliers, equipment and software.** Wholesale latex in
      the Pacific Northwest, inflators, frames, transport, storage, and
      quoting, CRM, contracts and payment tools for small event vendors.
- [x] **10 Business setup in Oregon.** Assumed business name, city and county
      licences, liability insurance for balloon and decor artists (providers,
      costs, certificates), contracts and standard clauses, deposits, taxes
      (Oregon has no sales tax; the Corporate Activity Tax threshold), tent
      permits.
- [x] **11 Business-to-business buyers on the coast.** Hotels and resorts, the
      casino, wineries and breweries, real estate (open houses, brokerages),
      car dealers, banks, retail, schools, nonprofits and civic events: who
      buys decor, when, and how to reach them.
- [x] **12 Craft, credentials and community.** Certified Balloon Artist and
      manufacturer education, conventions, online communities, mentorship,
      and case studies of balloon artists who grew on Instagram or TikTok.
