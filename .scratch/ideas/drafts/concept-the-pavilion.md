# Concept: The Pavilion

*A one-page product concept from the research, for the owner to react to.
**Not a decision.** It corresponds to tent prototype variant B and ideas #40–#45
in `../ideas.md`. Every price below is a placeholder: the pricing basis is
offering-model ticket 05.*

## The pitch

> **Your land. Our tent. Finished.**
> The 20×40 tent and a balloon installation, set up on your lawn, rental
> house or family land. A venue for up to about 60 guests seated, without
> the venue.

## Who it's for

- Couples planning a small coast wedding (25–60 guests) at a rental house or
  family property. The research found coast weddings mostly happen at
  resorts or at *non-venues*, and no event barns on the central coast.
- Milestone parties in big rental houses: 50th anniversaries, 40th
  birthdays, reunions.
- Valley families (about half of coast visitors come from Portland and the
  Willamette Valley) who own or rent at the coast.

## What's included

| Included | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| The 20×40 frame tent, anchored for coastal wind | Staked on lawn; ballasted on hard ground. [her call: confirm what her tent supports.] |
| One balloon installation inside (garland or ceiling "cloud") and one entrance piece | The ceiling can be priced per tent bay (idea #42) |
| Setup the morning of, strike the next day | The second day can carry a brunch restyle at a discount (idea #44) |
| The fire permit handled | Tents over 400 sq ft generally need a fire-department permit and an NFPA 701 certificate in Oregon. [her call: confirm the tent's certificate.] |
| Insurance certificate for the property owner or venue | $1M/$2M general liability, additional insured on request |
| Leave-no-trace teardown | Every fragment collected; nothing released |

**Not included (the client arranges, or Poppies refers):** tables and chairs
(referral swap with Oregon Coast Event Rental or Newport Rental Service, idea
#136), catering, restrooms, and any county permits beyond the tent.

## Price shape (placeholders)

- **From $[__]**, compared with a $3,000–15,000 venue fee, which is the
  comparison venue-in-a-box businesses sell on.
- Travel by zone along Highway 101 (idea #26).
- Peak (June–September) and shoulder (May, October) rates.

## What has to be true first

1. **The tent's real facts:** its flame-resistance certificate, its weight and
   anchoring kit, and how long one or two people take to put it up. (Tent
   ticket 07, checklist question 9.)
2. **Insurance** that covers tent installation, not only decor.
3. **A wind rule:** a published threshold above which the setup changes
   (idea #43).
4. **One real install**, photographed, before it's advertised. A styled shoot
   would do (idea #117).

## Open questions for her

- Would she do two-day jobs (set up, then strike the next day) in peak
  season, when that blocks a weekend?
- Does she want to handle permits, or only advise on them?
- Is a second person available to put the tent up?
