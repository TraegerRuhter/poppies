# A year on the coast: the 12-month calendar

*Maps the central coast's demand calendar to what Poppies should be doing each
month: outreach, content and launches. Built from `../research/03-oregon-coast-market.md`
(events, seasonality, weddings, schools), `../research/04-reviews-and-local-marketing.md`
(wedding shows, styled shoots) and `../ideas.md`. Event dates are from search
snippets and **move year to year; confirm each one.***

**The shape of the year:**

- **Weddings:** peak June–September, shoulder May and October.
- **Schools:** proms and graduations April–June.
- **Kite festivals:** June and September.
- **Holidays:** late November–December.
- **The quiet month:** February, except for the Newport Seafood & Wine Festival.
- **Rain:** about 270 mm a month in winter, so indoor and tent work carries
  October–April.

The rule of thumb: **sell each season about three months before it arrives.**
Weddings book months ahead, and B2B buyers plan holidays in autumn.

## The three heaviest lifts, placed

1. **The styled shoot (March).** Shoulder-season light, vendors free on
   weekdays, and images ready to pitch before wedding season.
2. **B2B holiday sales (August–September).** Hotels and shops decide holiday
   decor in late summer, while she's busiest with weddings. Put it in the
   calendar now, so it isn't forgotten.
3. **The wedding-pro mixer in the tent (#80).** April, before the season, or
   October, after it. Both are dry enough with the tent.

## Content cadence that survives peak season

- In peak months (June–September), stick to the minimum: one "Empty →
  Poppies" Reel per install, collab-posted. Nothing else is required.
- In quiet months (November–March), write the explainers and case studies,
  plan the shoot, and run outreach.
