# What does a balloon installation cost?

*Draft explainer for the website. Suggested home: `/guides/pricing`, linked
from the enquiry form. This draft explains what drives the price **without
publishing numbers**. Whether to publish "starting at" figures is an open

Every piece we make is designed for one room and one day, so we quote each
event individually. You shouldn't have to guess, though. This is what moves
the price, and roughly where things start.

## What moves the price

- **Length and size.** Garlands and arches are measured by the foot. A
  10-foot garland behind a cake table and a 30-foot run along a reception wall
  are very different jobs.
- **Fullness.** A light, airy garland uses fewer balloons than a lush,
  layered one. A statement piece uses many more, in more sizes.
- **Finish.** Matte and pastel colours, chrome, double-layered colour
  ("double-stuffed"), large feature balloons and greenery or florals each add
  materials and build time.
- **Height and difficulty.** Ceilings, stairways, ceremony arches and anything
  that needs a frame or rigging take longer to install safely.
- **Sculpture.** One-off figures are priced by design and build time, not by
  the foot.
- **Travel.** We're on the central coast. Longer drives along Highway 101 are
  a line on the quote. [her call: the travel zones and fees.]
- **Teardown and timing.** Same-night collection, early-morning installs and
  multi-day events all change the schedule.
- **The tent.** If you'd like the 20×40 tent, it's priced separately. [her
  call: depends on the tent ticket's outcome.]
