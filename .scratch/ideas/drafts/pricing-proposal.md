# Pricing proposal: a starting price card

*Research input to offering-model ticket 05 (pricing basis). **Not a
decision**; every number is for her to test. Built from
`../research/07-pricing-and-unit-economics.md` §6, about 75 price points from
search snippets, plus a cost build-up. Where the card fills in the `$[__]`
placeholders in other drafts, it says so.*

## The one thing to understand first

**Balloons are cheap; her time and the drive are not.** Materials for a
standard organic garland come to about $2.20 a foot, under 10% of a $28/ft
price. A 20 ft, $560 garland in Newport with same-day teardown takes about
10 hours with the driving, which pays about **$43 an hour before
overhead**. So the levers are the **minimum, travel and teardown fees**, more
than the per-foot rate. (`research/07` §2g)

## The proposed card

| Product | Proposed price | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Organic garland: standard** | **$28/ft** | The market middle; valley anchor about $25/ft (Pop & Go) |
| **Organic garland: lush or chrome** | **$35/ft** | Takes about 30% more time and material; keeps the margin |
| **Organic garland: with florals or foils** | **$42–45/ft** | Matches the premium tier nationally |
| **Organic arch, 8–10 ft, installed** | **$375–550** | Organic arches carry a 1.75–1.9× premium over classic |
| **Ceiling cloud** | **From $450** (small room); **from $1,200–1,500** (statement) | The only published structure found; quote on area and height |
| **Sculpture and commissions** | **Hours × $60–75, plus materials**; from $250 | A detail ladder: simple, detailed, gallery |
| **The Arrival** (rental surprise) | **$395 with collection**; $295 without, or for property-manager batches; $550+ premium | Fully costed at about $340 with collection; $250 only works without a return trip |
| **Proposal setup** | **$650–1,200**, in tiers (about $750 core; $1,200+ with a photographer) | PNW proposal packages are $495–1,450 |
| **Grab-and-go garland** | **$15–18/ft** (about $120–150 for 8 ft) | Half the installed price, low travel; good off-season |
| **Storefront subscription** | **$200–275/month** for a 10 ft refresh, 3-month minimum, skip with 7 days' notice | Real terms from two US studios; group shops into one route day |
| **The Pavilion** (tent + decor) | **About $3,000–4,500 all-in** | Tent at cost plus 15–25%, with decor at $1,200–2,000. **Get a real coast tent quote first** |

## Fees and terms

| Term | Proposed | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Minimum** | **$500** in Lincoln County; $650 Tillamook or Florence; $800+ inland | A single coast job uses most of a day; Seattle's minimums run $500–695 |
| **Travel** | **Zones**, not per mile: free Lincoln City to Yachats. If per mile, $1.50 beyond the free zone (about 2× the IRS 76¢) | Zones read better for remote clients |
| **Teardown** | **A separate fee: $95–150 local**, more at distance | The second round trip is about $76 of mileage and 1.5–2 hours |
| **Rush** (inside 7 days) | **15–20%, minimum $50** | A solo maker's rush displaces another job |
| **Deposit** | **50% non-refundable**, transferable once with 30 days' notice | The most common term found. Oregon voids non-refundable deposits only when they're grossly out of proportion (`research/10`) |
| **Helium** | **Add-on, about $2–3 per balloon** | Gas costs more than the latex, and supply is tight in 2026 |

## The checks to run on every quote

1. **Price ≥ 4–5× (materials + mileage).**
2. **Effective owner rate ≥ $45–60 an hour**, counting build, install,
   teardown and driving.

If a quote fails either check, raise the price or the minimum, or add the
travel or teardown fee.

## Where this departs from the market, and why

- **Teardown as its own line.** Many vendors bundle it, but city vendors don't
  drive 25 miles each way. On the coast it's the difference between a wage and
  a hobby.
- **A minimum that rises with distance.** Coast geography makes a flat minimum
  unfair to her.
- **Air-filled by default.** Most vendors price helium in. Here it's an
  add-on, which is honest about cost and fits the responsible-studio position.

## Keep an entry point

The *median* US wedding is about $10,000, not the $34,200 average, and kids'
parties average $314. Keep one package under $700 (a single focal garland at
the minimum, or grab-and-go), so the card isn't all premium.
