# Balloons and the coast

*Draft explainer for the website. Suggested home: `/guides/balloons-and-the-coast`.
It's also the long form of the "responsible balloon promise" below.*

We work beside the ocean, and so do the seabirds, seals and turtles that live
here. Balloons that escape can end up in the sea, so it's fair to ask whether
decorating with them is responsible. Here's our straight answer.

## What we promise

[her call: every line below is a proposed policy. Confirm each one before
publishing.]

1. **Nothing is ever released.** We don't do balloon releases, and we'll talk
   you out of one kindly.
2. **Everything is anchored.** Every piece is weighted or fixed to its frame,
   indoors and out.
3. **No foil balloons outdoors unless they're secured.** Loose metallic
   balloons can cause power outages as well as litter.
4. **We take it all away.** At teardown we collect every balloon and every
   fragment, and check the ground before we leave.
5. **We reuse what we can.** Frames, bases, weights and the tent are used
   again and again.

## Why we don't say "biodegradable"

You'll see latex balloons sold as "biodegradable" or "eco-friendly". We don't
use those words, because the evidence doesn't support them in any useful sense.

- In a 2020 peer-reviewed study, latex balloons, including ones labelled
  biodegradable, kept their shape and size after 16 weeks in seawater, fresh
  water and compost.
- One of the largest balloon manufacturers says it described latex as
  biodegradable in the dictionary sense, and that there is no standard
  laboratory test that shows how quickly latex balloons break down.

So our promise is about what we *do*, not what the material does: nothing
escapes, and nothing is left behind.

## What to do instead of a release

If you want a moment when everyone looks up, try a garland send-off line, a
confetti cannon indoors, ribbon wands, or a photo with the whole party in
front of the installation. We're happy to design one.

## Rules on beaches and in parks

Rules vary by place. Oregon has no statewide ban on balloon releases (a 2025
bill didn't pass), but cities and parks can set their own. If your event is on
a beach or in a state park, we'll check the rules for that spot before we
quote. [her call: confirm she'll do this check.]
