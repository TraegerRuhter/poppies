# Partner outreach: who to contact first, and what to say

*First-contact emails for the four partner types with the most leverage on
the central coast. Names come from `../research/03-oregon-coast-market.md` §7
(search snippets). **Confirm each contact by phone or on their website
before sending.***

Ground rules for every message:

- Short. Real. One clear ask (usually a 15-minute call or a coffee).
- Attach or link two or three photos of her work. Once she has a gallery,
  [a link to the site].
- Never claim a portfolio she doesn't have: no "we've done dozens of coast
  weddings" until it's true.
- Referral fees are fine. If a partner recommends Poppies **publicly** and is
  paid for referrals, they should disclose it (the FTC's endorsement logic;
  see `../research/04` §7).

## 1. Vacation-rental property managers: "The Arrival"

**Who first:** Meredith Lodging (Newport, Depoe Bay, Lincoln City, Yachats;
local staff) · iTrip Central Oregon Coast · Pacific Retreats (Lincoln City)
· Bella Beach Vacation Rentals · Pacific View Lodging (Depoe Bay). Vacasa
and Fairly are bigger, with centralised processes, so try them after the
local firms.

**Why they'd say yes:** guests ask them for celebration help, and an add-on
gives them a better guest experience and possibly referral revenue. Nobody on
the coast offers this today.

**Subject:** A celebration add-on for your guests

> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm [her name], and I run Poppies, a balloon installation studio based on
> the central coast. Many of your guests come to celebrate: birthdays,
> anniversaries, proposals, reunions. I'd like to offer them a small,
> beautiful installation that's waiting when they walk in.
>
> How it would work:
> - The guest books directly with me at least [7] days ahead.
> - You give me a door code or key window on the day; I install before
>   check-in, and every piece is free-standing, with nothing attached to
>   walls or furniture.
> - I collect everything afterwards, at a time you choose.
> - [her call: a 10–15% referral fee on bookings that come from you, or a
>   listing on your guest add-ons page.]
>
> I've attached a few photos of my work. Would you have 15 minutes for a call
> next week to see whether this would suit your guests?
>
> [Her name] · Poppies · [phone] · [site]

**What she needs first:** a fixed-price Arrival package (idea #30), proof of
insurance, a clear "nothing attached, nothing left behind" policy, and a
one-page sheet for managers (idea #32).

## 2. Coast venues: preferred-vendor lists

**Who first:** Salishan Coastal Lodge (sales@scphotel.com, per their 2026
packages PDF) · Anchor Inn Resort (its package already includes a 20×20
tent) · Hallmark Resort Newport · Inn at Spanish Head · Surftides · Adobe
Resort (Yachats).

**Why they'd say yes:** a local, reliable decor vendor who knows their spaces
and the wind, carries insurance, and cleans up completely makes their events
easier.

**Subject:** Balloon installations for your weddings and events

> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm [her name], and I run Poppies, a balloon installation studio based in
> [town]. I'd like to be a vendor your couples and event clients can count on:
> organic garlands, ceremony arches and sculptural pieces, designed for your
> spaces and built for coastal wind.
>
> A few things venues tend to care about:
> - **Insurance:** I can send a certificate naming [venue] as additional
>   insured. [her call: confirm her policy.]
> - **Nothing left behind:** every piece is weighted or free-standing, and
>   I collect every balloon and fragment at teardown.
> - **No releases**, ever.
>
> I'd love to see [the lawn / ballroom / grotto] and put together a one-page
> sheet of what works in your space, so your coordinators have something to
> show clients. Could I stop by for 20 minutes some weekday?
>
> [Her name] · Poppies · [phone] · [site]

**Bring to the visit:** the venue kit (idea #47), the certificate of insurance,
and a printed mock-up of an install in their own space.

## 3. Elopement photographers: the portable backdrop

**Who first:** Rustic Bloom Photography (Lincoln City) · Wandering Weddings ·
J Cobb Photography · Alix Ann Loosle Photography · Celebrate Again (Cannon
Beach).

**Why they'd say yes:** their packages run about $1,700–6,600, and decor is
the one thing they rarely include. A packable, wind-rated backdrop makes
their photos better and their package worth more.

**Subject:** A ceremony backdrop for your coast elopements

> Hi [Name],
>
> I love your coast work, especially [a specific shoot]. I'm [her name] from
> Poppies, a balloon studio based in [town].
>
> I've been designing a small, packable ceremony backdrop for elopements: a
> free-standing frame with an organic garland in muted coastal tones. It's
> built for wind, goes up in about 20 minutes, and leaves nothing behind.
> [her call: confirm the design and setup time once it's built.]
>
> Would you be interested in offering it as an add-on to your elopement
> packages? I'd happily set one up for one of your sessions or a portrait
> day, in exchange for photos, so you can see whether it suits your style.
>
> [Her name] · Poppies · [phone] · [site]

**Note:** a free install in exchange for photos is fine. Asking for a review
in exchange would break Google's rules (see `review-kit.md`).

## 4. The chambers: ribbon cuttings

**Who first:** the Greater Newport Chamber (541-265-8801; runs ribbon
cuttings, the Seafood & Wine Festival and the boat parade) · the Lincoln City
Chamber (4039 NW Logan Rd).

**Why they'd say yes:** they hold ribbon cuttings all year, and a standard,
flat-priced backdrop makes every new member's photo look better.

**Subject:** A standard backdrop for your ribbon cuttings

> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm [her name], and I run Poppies, a balloon installation studio in [town].
> I'd like to offer members a simple ribbon-cutting package: a garland
> backdrop in the business's colours and an oversized ribbon, set up and
> collected, at one flat price of $[__]. [her call: price.]
>
> Could it be mentioned to new members when you schedule their ribbon
> cutting? I'm also happy to join the chamber. [her call.]
>
> [Her name] · Poppies · [phone] · [site]
