# Concept: The Arrival

*A one-page product concept from the research, for the owner to react to.
**Not a decision.** Ideas #30–#34 and #131 in `../ideas.md`. Prices are
placeholders except where the research found market figures.*

## The pitch

> **Arrive to a celebration.**
> A small installation waiting in your vacation rental when you walk in:
> birthdays, anniversaries, babymoons, proposals, reunions.

## Why it could work here

- **Nobody on the coast does it.** The only celebration setups found are
  Portland picnic and proposal companies that travel. (`research/03` §2)
- **It's a proven format elsewhere.** Balloon studios sell rental surprise
  setups on Airbnb Services and Greetwell for about $250–550, with around 7
  days' notice and a door code from the guest. (`research/02`)
- **The occasion sets the price.** The same arch sells for about $75 as
  "decorations" and $800–1,850 as a proposal package. (`research/02`)
- **The access exists.** A handful of property managers control most guest
  contact on the central coast: Meredith Lodging, iTrip, Vacasa, Pacific
  Retreats and others. (`research/03` §2)

## The product

| | |
| --- | --- |
| What | One sculptural piece or garland plus a printed card, all free-standing. Nothing attached to the rental's walls or furniture |
| When | Installed before check-in; collected at a time the manager chooses |
| Notice | [7] days. [her call] |
| Price | Fixed: $[__] for the Arrival; $[__] for the proposal version (idea #35) |
| How it's sold | Direct on the site; through property managers (referral fee [10–15]%, her call); later, Airbnb Services |
| Gift version | A certificate bought by family and friends (idea #34). Surprises are usually bought by someone other than the guest |

## What has to be true first

1. A standard free-standing design that one person can carry in and set up in
   under 30 minutes. [her call]
2. A one-page sheet for property managers: terms, net price, lead time
   (idea #32). The first-contact email is in `partner-outreach.md`.
3. Insurance that covers work inside rental properties.
4. A clear collection policy, so cleaners never have to deal with balloons.
