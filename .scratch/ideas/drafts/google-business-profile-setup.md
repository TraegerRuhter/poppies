# Google Business Profile: setup checklist

*Google's AI "Ask" button replaced the old Q&A section in November 2025. It
answers customers' questions from the profile, the reviews and the website, so
this profile and the website's FAQ now do the answering. Sourcing is in
`../research/04-reviews-and-local-marketing.md` §1.*

**Who does this:** the owner, signed in with the business Google account
(decisions §4: registered in her name, not the developer's).

## Setup

- [ ] **Business name:** "Poppies" exactly. No keywords added ("Poppies
      Balloon Arches Lincoln City" breaks the guidelines). [Blocked until the
      trademark check on POPPIE (serial 98162236) is resolved. See
      `../../naming/poppies-name-collision.md`.]
- [ ] **Primary category:** *Balloon artist*. Confirm it in the dashboard,
      because Google changes the list without notice.
- [ ] **Secondary categories:** only those that describe real services. Check
      what exists: *Wedding service*, *Party planner*, *Event planner*,
      *Party equipment rental service* (for the tent, if it's offered).
- [ ] **Service-area business:** hide the home address. List the towns she
      actually serves by name (Google no longer allows a radius). Use the same
      list as the website footer, `src/content/service-area.yaml`, once it's
      confirmed.
- [ ] **Description:** plain words that answer the common questions: what she
      makes, the towns, weddings and celebrations, air-filled, everything
      anchored and collected.
- [ ] **Website link** with tracking: `https://[domain]/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp`.
- [ ] **Hours:** "by appointment", or the hours she answers enquiries.

## Photos (the cheap advantage)

- [ ] Upload every install, from several angles, as it happens. Aim for 1–2
      new photos a week. Balloon work photographs well.
- [ ] Include process shots, the tent (if it's on the menu), and her at work.
      That last one is the About-page portrait slot.
- [ ] No stock images.

## Reviews

- [ ] Get the official review link and QR code (Read reviews → Get more
      reviews → Share; the QR code is desktop only).
- [ ] Use the review kit (`review-kit.md`) for every client.
- [ ] Reply to every review.

## Monthly, in five minutes

- [ ] In Performance, check searches, calls and website clicks. Ignore raw
      impressions.
- [ ] Count new reviews.
- [ ] Post one update (season, a recent install). Posts are freshness, not a
      ranking lever.
