# How long will my balloons last?

*Draft explainer for the website. Suggested home: an FAQ page or `/guides/how-long`.*

The honest answer depends on three things: whether the balloons are filled with
air or helium, whether they're indoors or out, and the weather. On the coast,
the weather matters more than most places.

## The short version

| Where | Air-filled garland or arch | Helium |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Indoors | Several days at their best. They gradually soften to a matte finish after that. | Hours, unless treated. |
| Under a tent | Built for the whole event, and usually the day after too. | Hours. |
| Outdoors in sun or wind | Built for the day of your event. | Not recommended. |

Most of what we make is **air-filled**, on purpose. Air-filled pieces don't
float, but they last far longer, cope better with heat and damp, and can't
drift away.

## What shortens a balloon's life

- **Sun.** Latex reacts to light and heat. Glossy colours turn soft and matte,
  and chrome finishes dull fastest. We choose palettes that still look right
  once that happens.
- **Heat.** Warm air expands, and a balloon filled to the limit can pop in the
  sun. Outdoors, we fill slightly under size, favour lighter colours, and keep
  pieces in shade where we can.
- **Cold.** Cold air contracts, so balloons look softer on a chilly morning and
  fill out again as the day warms up. Nothing is wrong when that happens.
- **Damp.** Coastal humidity shortens helium float times considerably, and can
  weaken the adhesive that holds some pieces in place. It's one more reason we
  build with air and fix pieces mechanically.
- **Wind.** A large piece catches wind like a sail. Everything we install
  outdoors is anchored and weighted, and on exposed sites we'll suggest where
  to put it, or suggest the tent.

## What about pops?

An organic garland is designed so that one or two pops are invisible: the
balloons are packed in clusters of different sizes. We bring spares to every
install. [her call: say whether she offers a touch-up visit for multi-day
events.]

If a balloon does pop, pick up the pieces straight away. Broken balloon is a
choking hazard for small children and harmful to pets if swallowed.

## Planning around it

- **Indoor celebrations:** we can install the day before.
- **Outdoor ceremonies on the coast:** we install the morning of the event.
  If the forecast turns, we'll talk about moving the piece or using the tent.
