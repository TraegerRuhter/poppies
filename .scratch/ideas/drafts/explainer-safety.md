# Safety at your event: children, pets and allergies

*Draft explainer for the website. Suggested home: an FAQ section or
`/guides/safety`.*

Balloons are safe to enjoy with a little care. These are the three things
worth knowing.

## Small children

Inflated balloons aren't the hazard. **Broken balloons and uninflated
balloons are.** Children under 8 can choke on balloon pieces, so:

- If a balloon pops, pick up every piece straight away.
- Don't let small children chew or play with deflated balloons.
- At the end of the event, leave the takedown to an adult, or to us.

## Pets

Dogs and cats sometimes eat balloon pieces, and larger pieces can cause a
blockage. Keep pets away from popped balloons. If a pet swallows some and
starts vomiting or stops eating, call your vet.

## Latex allergies

Most of what we make is latex, which is natural rubber. Latex allergy affects
a small share of people (estimates run from about 1 to 4 percent), and some
react to latex in the air, not just on contact. That's why many hospitals
don't allow latex balloons.

**If any guest has a latex allergy, tell us when you enquire.** We can design
with foil or other latex-free materials instead. [her call: confirm she offers
