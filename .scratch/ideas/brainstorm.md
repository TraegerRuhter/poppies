# Brainstorm: unfiltered ideas

2026-11-06. **These are Claude's own ideas, not research findings.** They come
from what this repo already knows: the tent (`../offering-model/issues/07-tent-role.md`),
the restrained brand (`../website/decisions.md`), the coast, the pricing and
packaging research, and the site that's been built. They are deliberately
unfiltered. Triage happens in `ideas.md`, where these are merged with the
research-backed ideas.

## Products and formats

1. **The Backyard Venue.** The tent plus decor on the customer's land, sold as
   one product (prototype variant B). It turns "we have a yard and a guest
   list" into a booking.
2. **Signature pieces with names.** Three or four fixed designs ("The Driftwood
   Arch", "The Tidepool Garland"), photographed once and sold repeatedly at a
   fixed price. Custom work turned into products.
3. **The Coast Palette collection.** A seasonal colour story (fog, kelp,
   sea-glass, driftwood, dune grass) released each spring, so the brand owns
   "coastal colour".
4. **Grab-and-go garland boxes.** Pre-built 6 ft and 10 ft garlands in boxes,
   picked up from a fixed spot on set days. A low-touch product for small
   parties.
5. **Statement piece only.** One sculptural centrepiece, no garlands. The
   gallery-art version of balloon decor, aimed at people who'd never hire a
   "balloon person".
6. **Balloon portraiture.** A commissioned sculpture of a family pet or a
   couple's initials as a keepsake piece, photographed professionally for them.
7. **Arrival moments for vacation rentals.** A small installation waiting when
   guests arrive for a birthday or anniversary week, booked via the property
   manager.
8. **Proposal setups.** A beach-adjacent or rental-deck proposal backdrop,
   timed for golden hour, with a photographer partner. High emotion,
   small build, premium price.
9. **Elopement kit.** A small arch and floor piece for two people and a
   photographer. The coast is an elopement destination.
10. **The second-day piece.** Re-style the same installation the next morning
    for a brunch or send-off, at a discount. It uses the second day the tent
    is already up.
11. **Corporate brand colours.** Grand openings, office anniversaries, bank and
    casino promos, built in the client's brand colours. Weekday work, and repeat
    customers.
12. **Storefront seasons.** A quarterly window or entrance installation for
    downtown shops (Nye Beach, Bay Front, Cannon Beach galleries) on a simple
    retainer.
13. **Holiday installs.** A December window or lobby piece for hotels and
    lodges. One heavy month every year.
14. **Graduation and prom.** School contracts in May and June. Lots of volume
    in the shoulder season.
15. **Memorial and celebration-of-life pieces.** A quiet, dignified palette,
    handled tactfully. Underserved, and fits the restrained brand.
16. **Gender-neutral baby-shower line.** A fix for the cliché pink and blue.
17. **Rental-only kit.** Reusable frames and backdrops rented out for customers
    to dress themselves, with or without a balloon add-on.
18. **Air-filled only, as a policy.** No helium, ever. It's cheaper, lasts
    longer, avoids shortages, and nothing can float away. Sold as a design
    choice, not a limitation.

## Pricing and packaging

19. **Publish "starting at" prices.** No coastal vendor does, so being the
    only one to show prices is a position in itself.
20. **A three-tier menu with finish levels** (packaging research), with the
    tent only at the top.
21. **Off-peak pricing.** Lower weekday and winter rates to fill the quiet
    season.
22. **A travel-zone map.** Free within zone 1, then a flat fee per zone. It
    makes Highway 101 distances predictable.
23. **Date-hold deposit.** A small non-refundable hold for peak-season
    Saturdays. It filters out serious buyers.
24. **Gift certificates.** Sold for birthdays and anniversaries, they lock in
    revenue and spread word of mouth.
25. **A loyalty "second celebration" credit.** Repeat clients (birthdays recur
    yearly) get a credit toward their next one.
26. **Bundle with partners.** Balloons, florals and a photographer as one
    quote, with each vendor billing separately.

## Website and tools

27. **A garland length estimator.** "How much garland do I need?" with
    illustrations: mantel, cake table, doorway, arch.
28. **A quote estimator.** A price range from the event type, size and town,
    which also pre-qualifies the enquiry.
29. **A palette builder.** Pick three colours and see them as balloon swatches,
    then send the palette with the enquiry.
30. **Before/after slider gallery.** An empty room, then the finished room.
31. **"Is my venue windy?" guide.** Outdoor coast advice by town and season.
32. **A booking-season tracker.** "June 2027: 3 Saturdays left." Honest
    scarcity, updated by hand.
33. **A care card.** A QR code on each install links to a care page (keep it
    out of sun and heaters, what to expect, how to dispose of it responsibly).
34. **A "how it works" timeline**: enquiry, then design call, deposit,
    install and teardown.
35. **A case-study page for each install**: the brief, the palette, the space,
    the result.
36. **The shot list as a content engine.** Every install produces the same five
    shots for the gallery automatically.

## Reviews and trust

37. **A review moment built into teardown.** Leave a printed card with a QR
    code at the peak emotional moment, before the party.
38. **Photo delivery as a review prompt.** Send the client their professional
    photos with a review link in the same message.
39. **Planner and venue testimonials.** Trade quotes from the people who
    refer, not just the people who book.
40. **A no-surprises guarantee.** The quote is the price; an install is
    on time or the fee is reduced. That's a trust signal, but only if she can
    stand behind it.
41. **Responsible-balloon pledge.** No releases, latex collected after events,
    honest material claims.

## Reach and partnerships

42. **Preferred-vendor lists.** Get onto the lists of every coastal lodge and
    event venue.
43. **Property-manager partnership.** A referral fee or listing in the welcome
    book of vacation rentals.
44. **A photographer collab day.** A styled shoot with three local
    photographers. Everyone gets portfolio images.
45. **A planner open house.** A one-afternoon showcase install, with local
    planners invited to see the work in person.
46. **A chamber of commerce membership**, which also opens grand-opening work.
47. **A public piece every season.** One large free installation at a visible
    spot (festival, library, aquarium entrance). It's an advert people photograph.
48. **A charity build.** Donated decor for a local fundraiser gala. Goodwill
    and visibility.
49. **Kite festival tie-in.** Lincoln City is kite country; a balloon-kite
    sculpture is very photographable.

## Content and explainers

50. **Time-lapse builds.** A 30-second Reel for every install. This is the format
    balloon artists win with.
51. **"Why a garland costs what it costs."** A transparent breakdown of
    materials, hours and travel.
52. **A latex vs foil vs chrome explainer.**
53. **"How long will it last?"** An honest guide to indoors vs outdoors, sun and
    cold.
54. **"Balloons and the coast."** A responsible-use explainer: no releases,
    wildlife, what happens to latex.
55. **A palette of the month post.**
56. **A behind-the-scenes series**: the studio, the materials, the one-person
    reality. People book people.

## Bold

57. **A balloon art piece in a gallery.** Enter a coastal art walk or a show with
    a sculptural balloon work. It makes "pop art for the coast" literal.
58. **A seasonal pop-up shop in a vacant storefront** for gifts and grab-and-go.
59. **The tent as a roaming "celebration room"** at markets and festivals,
    where people book a photo moment in it.
60. **Teaching**: an annual garland workshop for brides and party hosts, sold
    as a night out.
61. **A licensed "Coast Palette" product**, such as palette cards or kits sold
    in local gift shops.
62. **A drone-shot hero install** for every big build. Aerial images of a
    garland on a bluff stop the scroll.
63. **Partner with a winery or brewery** for a monthly event installation in
    their tasting room.
64. **Sponsor a local sports team or school play.** A backdrop for photos at
    their big night.
65. **The "Poppies Pop-Up Party".** One free small install drawn by lottery
    each month for a local cause or person. Shareable, and builds a mailing list.
