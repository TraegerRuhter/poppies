# Adjacent niches: new event/party businesses of the last ~10 years and what Poppies can borrow

Date: 2026-11-06

## How this was sourced
- Built from about 30 web searches on 2026-11-06. Direct page fetching was mostly blocked, so **almost every claim comes from search-result snippets and summaries, not from reading the full pages.** Prices especially may be out of date or quoted out of context, so check the live page before relying on one.
- Primary sources (the business's own site, local news, trade press) were preferred; listicles and SEO-spam pages were dropped. Marketplace listings (Airbnb, Batch, The Knot, Zola, WeddingWire) are self-reported by vendors.
- Confidence tags: **high** = primary source or several sources agree; **medium** = one credible source, or a vendor's own claims; **low** = thin, indirect, or the vendor is unclear.
- "Last ~10 years" is applied loosely. A few category-defining firms are older (The Yes Girls 2008, Heart Bandits 2010) but are included because they defined categories that grew after 2016.

## 1. Luxury / pop-up picnics
- **The Picnic Collective (Los Angeles):** started 2019 by Lauren Rivard. $399 for two; "Simple Pleasures" $499 (up to 6) / $1,199 (up to 18); top jobs past $10,000. Celebrity and influencer clients. [Business Insider](https://businessinsider.nl/i-throw-luxury-private-picnics-that-cost-upwards-of-10000-here-are-some-of-the-wildest-things-clients-have-asked-for-and-what-makes-for-the-best-experience/). Confidence: medium-high.
- **Alpaca Picnic (St. Louis):** Julie Ellison, started in the pandemic. From $169 for two, 2-hour rental. [KSDK](https://ksdk.com/article/entertainment/television/show-me-st-louis/alpaca-picnic/63-8580ee89-c46d-461e-9114-9e1a00912e31). Confidence: medium.
- **Airbnb Services picnic listings:** Pop Up Picnic NYC $400/group ([Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/services/7046418)); Suite Life Studios $45/guest, max 10 ([Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/services/7052191)); Breezy Picnic LA from $80-90/guest ([Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/services/7190168)). Confidence: medium (self-listed prices).
- **Wild Rose Picnics (Lake Oswego, OR):** serves Portland, Willamette Valley, Mt Hood, and the Oregon Coast. [The Knot](https://www.theknot.com/marketplace/wild-rose-picnics-lake-oswego-or-2072688). Confidence: medium.
- **Overleaf Lodge & Spa (Yachats, OR):** sells "Picnics by the Sea" as a lodge add-on. [Unique Inns](https://www.uniqueinns.com/specials/overleaf-lodge-and-spa/picnics-by-the-sea-8207). Confidence: medium.
- **TikTok was the growth engine (2020-21):** a planner covered by GMA said a viral TikTok let her charge up to $3,000 an event ([GMA/ABC](https://abcnews.go.com/food/story/luxury-picnic-planners-share-top-tips-create-stunning-77337545)); Foothills Picnics (CO) said 60% of its bookings come through social media; Picnic 'n Chill went viral on TikTok and then got GMA and CNBC coverage; a Harford County MD company "grew out of pandemic, TikTok videos" ([Yahoo/Aegis](https://news.yahoo.com/luxury-picnic-company-grew-pandemic-125900587.html)); a Cal Poly student started one after seeing the trend on TikTok ([KCPR](https://kcpr.org/?p=14048)); Houston coverage ([Houstonia](https://www.houstoniamag.com/eat-and-drink/the-big-business-of-picnics/)). Confidence: medium-high.
- Staying power: Newport RI picnic businesses still going post-pandemic ([What's Up Newp](https://whatsupnewp.com/2022/05/pop-up-picnics-businesses-prove-they-have-staying-power/)); Smithsonian trend piece ([Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/innovation/picnics-are-back-180982102/)).

## 2. Micro-weddings / elopement packages
- **Simply Eloped:** founded by Janessa White (co-founder Matt Dalley); later bought by The Knot Worldwide. Elopements up to 15 guests, micro-weddings up to 50. [Simply Eloped team page](https://simplyeloped.com/team/), [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/simply-eloped). Confidence: high.
- **Pop-Up Weddings Calgary (2015, Erin Bjorkstedt):** three tiers, about CA$2,600 to $5,200. Rents one venue for a day and runs up to five couples back to back, splitting the venue cost. [Avenue Calgary](https://www.avenuecalgary.com/avenue-calgary-weddings/pop-up-weddings-calgary). Confidence: high.
- **Just Elope (Dallas, Jennifer Allen):** pitched micro-weddings to venues as a way to fill gaps in their calendars. [Black Enterprise](https://blackenterprise.com/black-woman-owned-micro-wedding-company-shares-5-tips-budget-friendly-wedding/). Confidence: medium.
- **PopWed Co. (DC):** an officiant and a photographer run quick weddings in public spaces. [Big Think](https://bigthink.com/?p=55660). Confidence: medium.
- **Evented (Edmonton):** pop-up elopement startup. [Edmonton Journal via PressReader](https://www.pressreader.com/canada/edmonton-journal/20170513/282595967835081). Confidence: medium.
- **Elope in Austin:** [Community Impact](https://communityimpact.com/austin/lake-travis-westlake/arts-entertainment/2022/04/18/local-wedding-business-elope-in-austin-redefines-weddings). Confidence: medium.
- Destination prices: Palm Springs all-inclusive $5,000 for up to 20 guests ([Visit Palm Springs PDF](https://assets.simpleviewinc.com/simpleview/image/upload/v1/clients/palmsprings/Micro_Wedding_Brochure_186cfd42-51a0-4fe4-82e7-6603edb1bd08.pdf)); Hilton Head from $5,000 ([Hilton Head](https://hiltonheadisland.org/offers/micro-wedding)). Confidence: high.

## 3. Backyard wedding / tent-plus-decor bundles
- Arizona "Backyard Wedding Bundle" $1,810 plus delivery: up to 10 farm tables, 400 ft of string lights, 16x16 dance floor, setup included. [My Event Rentals](https://reach.dog/shop/my-event-rentals/products/intimate-outdoor-wedding-package). Confidence: medium.
- MN/WI backyard-wedding planner sells tiered packages; tent rental billed separately; the top tier "reserves our entire decor inventory for one couple." [Squarespace packages page](https://celery-cornet-f4wb.squarespace.com/packages). Confidence: low-medium (vendor unclear).

- **Patty Wagon (Tri-Cities WA):** two sisters converted a 40-year-old stock trailer; $600 for 4 hours. [Tri-Cities Area Journal of Business](https://www.tricitiesbusinessnews.com/2019/07/patty-wagon-touring-taps/). Confidence: high.
- **Wild Horse Mobile Bar:** started from a $400 horse trailer after a missed booking; five seasons in. [The Preferred List podcast](https://www.buzzsprout.com/2520237/episodes/18743572-episode-33-how-a-rusty-trailer-accidentally-became-a-mobile-bar-wild-horse-mobile-bar). Confidence: medium.
- **Sipping Southern (Williamsburg VA):** couple, 1984 horse trailer, launched while still working day jobs. [WYDaily](https://wydaily.com/brews-and-bites/2022/02/18/williamsburg-couple-turns-vintage-horse-trailer-into-mobile-bar-to-serve-parties-events/). Confidence: high.
- **Bars Without Boundaries (Saratoga Springs):** bar and cafe. [WNYT](https://wnyt.com/top-stories/wnyt-upstate-of-business/horse-trailer-turned-into-mobile-bar-and-cafe); Metro Detroit example ([Fox 2](https://www.fox2detroit.com/news/metro-detroit-woman-transforms-old-horse-trailer-into-mobile-bar-for-parties)). Confidence: high.
- Most of these bring the trailer and service but not the alcohol (the host buys it), which avoids liquor licensing.

## 7. 360 booths / selfie walls
- 360 booth rentals mostly run $600-1,500 per event; booths cost $4k-8k; part-timers do 2-4 events a month. Figures come from suppliers with an interest in the numbers. [Photo Booth Supply Co](https://blog.photoboothsupplyco.com/photo-booth-equipment-costs-roi-and-what-operators-actually-earn), [Check Cherry](https://www.checkcherry.com/articles/235-how-much-does-photo-booth-rental-c). Confidence: low-medium.
- **Flower Wall Bermuda (Catarina Rocha):** silk-flower backdrop rental. Launched with five walls, took off as soon as lockdown lifted, and added a photo booth and then float rentals. [Royal Gazette](https://www.royalgazette.com/?p=162441). Confidence: medium.
- Selfie studios (fixed-location walls rented by the hour) spread 2019-22: Myrtle Beach Selfie WRLD, St Pete Social Warehouse, and others. [Post and Courier](https://www.postandcourier.com/myrtle-beach/business/selfie-wrld-a-unique-selfie-studio-in-myrtle-beach-set-for-april-opening/article_ec2559fe-93c4-11eb-a0da-e3fc5a3cfb88.html), [St Pete Rising](https://stpeterising.com/home/2021/8/18/st-petes-first-selfie-studio-the-social-warehouse-will-debut-this-fall-in-downtown-st-pete). Confidence: medium.
- A DFW turnkey 360 business listed at $15k revenue, $11k SDE: the commodity end. [BizQuest](https://images.bizquest.com/start-up-business/photobooth-and-360-booth-20-000-turnkey-business/BW2381776). Confidence: medium.

## 8. Kids' teepee sleepovers / party concierge
- **AZ Sleepy Teepee (Arizona, 2018, Dana Maciag):** started from her daughter's 10th birthday sleepover and had its first two paying clients within weeks. Now trademarked and offering national licences. [Arizona Foothills](https://www.arizonafoothillsmagazine.com/valleygirlblog/?p=32068), [Macaroni Kid](https://southscottsdale.preview.macaronikid.com/articles/69fbefb02bc26d73308ec402/arizona-families-are-redefining-sleepovers). Confidence: medium.
- **Elite Teepees (Nikki Kay):** licence model, no franchise fees or royalties. [PR Newswire](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/elite-teepees-offering-unique-business-license-opportunity-300993520.html). Confidence: medium (press release).
- **Teepee Nights (Sydney):** [SmartCompany](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/entrepreneurs/business-idea-kids-birthday-parties/). **Sleepy Teepee Fun (UK, 2020):** launched with 4 tents and 2 themes. [Storage King](https://www.storageking.co.uk/blog/this-teepee-specialist-is-taking-sleepovers-to-the-next-level/). **Slumber One Party (Main Line PA):** got the idea from an Etsy listing. [Main Line Tonight](https://mainlinetonight.com/the-new-main-line-sleepover/). Confidence: medium.
- The model: a fixed number of identical themed "sets," priced per tent, delivered and set up indoors and collected the next day. Each new theme is a cheap kit of linens and props on the same frames. Pinterest and Instagram spread the format, and licensing turns the founder's playbook into income.

## 9. Proposal planning
- **The Yes Girls (2008, Heather Vaughn):** started after finding about 100,000 monthly Google searches for proposal help and no businesses offering it. Packages ran from a $400 "idea" consult to VIP; later reporting puts the custom minimum at $5,000. [The Yes Girls about](https://theyesgirls.com/about-yes-local/), [InsideHook](https://www.insidehook.com/article/relationships/the-weird-decadent-world-of-marriage-proposal-consulting), [Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/wellness/proposal-planners). Confidence: high (founding), medium (prices).
- **The Heart Bandits (2010, co-founder Michele Velazquez):** claims 100% growth every year ([Money](https://money.com/valentines-creative-ways-propose-marriage/?amp=true)). The model is heavily productised: one fixed "Heart Arch" package repeated on a dedicated page for each city (Houston $799, Phoenix $999, LA $1,699, Hawaii $1,849), with priced add-ons (marquee letters $99-999, petals $100-375). Bookings need about 7 days' notice and are non-refundable. [Atlanta heart arch page](https://www.theheartbandits.com/atlanta-proposal-ideas/heart-arch-proposal-atlanta/), [Phoenix](https://theheartbandits.com/?p=48510), [Hawaii](https://theheartbandits.com/?p=47006). Confidence: high (prices from their own pages, may be dated).
- **Lesson:** the per-city, per-package pages are search-engine plumbing. Every "[city] proposal ideas" search lands on a fixed-price product with a photo. A balloon heart arch is central to their best seller.

## 10. Vacation-rental surprise setups / arrival experiences
- Local operators sell pre-arrival decor packages (birthday, bachelorette "bride reveal," girls' trips). The guest shares the check-in time and door code, and the setup is done before arrival. Many use disposable decor so nothing has to be collected. Mostly listed on **Batch** (bachelorette marketplace). [Batch listing example](https://letsbatch.com/experiences/5641fc42-8770-44dc-8983-4fb489e9785d), [another](https://letsbatch.com/experiences/0b80ab01-0891-4189-be5e-2c9008d6f84f). Confidence: medium.
- **Batch (formerly Bach):** says it has 1,600+ vendors in 25+ cities; vendors pay to list; Nashville is the top market. Its blog has a case study on a vendor reaching #1 on Google without ads. [Batch FAQ](https://letsbatch.com/faqs), [2023 report](https://letsbatch.com/blog/bach-app-special-report), [Rock n Boho case study](https://letsbatch.com/blog/how-batch-helped-rock-n-boho-hit-1-on-google-without-paid-ads), [suppliers page](https://letsbatch.com/suppliers). Confidence: medium (self-reported).
- Hosts don't owe guests decor ([Airbnb community](https://community.withairbnb.com/t5/Support-with-your-bookings/Thoughtful-details-guests-love/m-p/2035958)), so the opening is a third-party vendor the host or property manager approves and offers as an add-on.
- **Balloon artists are already selling on Airbnb Services:** Balloons by Candice (Miami) $250/group with "Bedroom" and "Mini Decor" packages ([Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/services/7009861)); Balloon styling by Aly, "Surprise balloon bundles" ([Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/services/6970374)); Joyful balloon designs by David $550/group ([Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/services/7207596)); Balloon decor by Vivi ([Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/services/7137431)); Balloon Celebrations ([Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/services/6867246)); Luxury Events LA from $415 ([Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/services/7101523)). Confidence: medium (snippet prices; the per-guest vs per-group unit varies by regional site).
- **Greetwell:** a B2B2C upsell marketplace sold to vacation-rental managers and boutique hotels. Local providers list add-ons that guests book before arrival, Greetwell collects payment and pays the vendor, and a "Celebration Decorations" balloon arch add-on is listed at $74.99 with 7 days' notice. [Greetwell for operators](https://www.greetwell.com/wf/for-tour-operators), [listing](https://www.greetwell.com/us/direct/8977), [Rigby case study](https://www.rigbyjs.com/case-studies/greetwell). Similar upsell stores exist in Hostfully, Hospitable, Hostaway, and StayFi ([Hostfully](https://www.hostfully.com/blog/increase-revenue-with-hostfully-guidebooks-marketplace), [Hospitable](https://hospitable.com/upsells-launch), [StayFi](https://stayfi.com/upsell-integrations/)). Confidence: medium-high.
- Resort versions: Westgate Vacation Villas sells balloon, flower, and champagne room-decor packages ([Westgate](https://www.westgateresorts.com/hotels/florida/orlando/westgate-vacation-villas-resort/overview/room-decorations/)). Confidence: high.
- **Airbnb Services (May 2025):** 10 categories (chefs, photography, catering, etc.); no "decor" category was found, but luxury picnic vendors appear listed (see section 1), probably under catering. [Airbnb newsroom](https://news.airbnb.com/en-au/2025-may-release-now-you-can-airbnb-more-than-an-airbnb/), [Hostaway](https://www.hostaway.com/blog/airbnb-2025-summer-release-everything-app/). Confidence: medium.
- Local precedent: Overleaf Lodge (Yachats) already sells picnic and gift add-on packages ([Unique Inns](https://www.uniqueinns.com/specials/overleaf-lodge-and-spa/picnics-and-gift-packages-8207)).

## 11. Glamping / event tent rentals
- **The Pop-Up BnB (Cincinnati, co-owner Kelly Emilianowicz):** bell tents as wedding guest lodging, bridal suites, and lounges on private land; from about $1,300 for delivery and setup; travels from Texas to Maine. [WeddingWire](https://weddingwire.com/biz/the-pop-up-bnb/7dffad8d1d8fbdb2.html), [Consistently Curious](https://www.consistentlycurious.com/glamping-in-your-backyard-with-pop-up-bnb/). Confidence: medium-high.
- **Beautiful Bells (UK, 2013):** builds bell-tent "villages" at weddings, and each **guest books and pays for their own tent** through a personalised page. [Add to Event](https://www.addtoevent.co.uk/beautiful-bells). Confidence: medium.
- **Twilight Event Hire (AU, 2015):** inspired by a UK glamping wedding; grew into marquees, furniture, and tipis. **Happy Glamper (AU, 2013):** one of the first mobile glamping hire businesses. [Easy Weddings](https://www.easyweddings.com.au/WeddingAccommodation/Melbourne/TwilightGlamping/), [Polka Dot Wedding](https://polkadotwedding.com/wedding-vendor/happy-glamper/). Confidence: medium.
- **San Juan Glamping (US):** started with one bell tent. [Boutique Camping trade Q&A](https://boutiquecamping.com/blogs/news/trade-testimonial-setting-up-an-events-rental-glamping-business). Confidence: medium.

## 13. Digital templates / DIY guides / kits
- **ElliesPartySupply (Etsy, Meg):** DIY balloon garland kits. When she started there were "a small handful" of kit shops; now there are "dozens, if not hundreds," so she is diversifying. [Alura interview](https://www.alura.io/interview/elliespartysupply). Confidence: high.
- **Little Agency Co (Gumroad):** balloon-business bundles (garland templates, menus, contracts, eBook) at $9.99-159.99, with few public ratings. [Gumroad](https://littleagencyco.gumroad.com/l/grandballoonartistbundle). Payhip 90-page balloon business guide ([Payhip](https://payhip.com/BalloonArtistProGuide)). Confidence: medium.
- **Coterie:** raised $2.75M (pre-seed led by Canaan) for a high-end shipped "party in a box" at about $86-320. [The Spoon](https://thespoon.tech/coterie-will-deliver-a-high-end-party-in-a-box-to-your-doorstep). **Happy Hippo Party Boxes (NC):** [ABC11](https://abc11.com/post/nc-made-happy-hippo-party-boxes-helps-parents-simplify-birthday-planning/19751965/). **The Box Babe (Baton Rouge):** themed party boxes for pickup or shipping. [225 Magazine](https://www.225batonrouge.com/?p=211090). Confidence: medium.
- Lesson: the DIY-kit space in balloons is saturated, and the B2B "teach other balloon artists" market is crowded too. A distinctive aesthetic that can be licensed (palettes, a named "look") is less contested than generic garland kits.


## 14. Productised balloon studios (direct comparables)
- **The Balloon Guy (LA, Sean Edwards):** grew from a mall kiosk to a "luxury event brand" known for sculptural work, celebrity clients, and eco-conscious practices. [Press release, Apr 2026](https://lifestyle.q923radio.com/story/155753/from-mall-kiosk-to-luxury-event-brand-the-balloon-guy-marks-major-growth-milestone/). Confidence: low-medium (self-published).
- **The Heart Bandits** (section 9) is in effect a productised balloon/neon arch business with proposal positioning, and its prices ($799-1,849 base) are far above what a plain "balloon arch" listing gets ($74.99 on Greetwell, $250 on Airbnb). **The occasion sets the price, not the balloons.**
