# Adjacent niches: new event/party businesses of the last ~10 years and what Poppies can borrow

Date: 2026-11-06

## How this was sourced
Work in progress. Sections fill in as the research goes.

## 1. Luxury / pop-up picnics
- **The Picnic Collective (Los Angeles):** started 2019 by Lauren Rivard. $399 for two; "Simple Pleasures" $499 (up to 6) / $1,199 (up to 18); top jobs past $10,000. Celebrity and influencer clients. [Business Insider](https://businessinsider.nl/i-throw-luxury-private-picnics-that-cost-upwards-of-10000-here-are-some-of-the-wildest-things-clients-have-asked-for-and-what-makes-for-the-best-experience/). Confidence: medium-high.
- **Alpaca Picnic (St. Louis):** Julie Ellison, started in the pandemic. From $169 for two, 2-hour rental. [KSDK](https://ksdk.com/article/entertainment/television/show-me-st-louis/alpaca-picnic/63-8580ee89-c46d-461e-9114-9e1a00912e31). Confidence: medium.
- **Airbnb Services picnic listings:** Pop Up Picnic NYC $400/group ([Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/services/7046418)); Suite Life Studios $45/guest, max 10 ([Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/services/7052191)); Breezy Picnic LA from $80-90/guest ([Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/services/7190168)). Confidence: medium (self-listed prices).
- **Wild Rose Picnics (Lake Oswego, OR):** serves Portland, Willamette Valley, Mt Hood, and the Oregon Coast. [The Knot](https://www.theknot.com/marketplace/wild-rose-picnics-lake-oswego-or-2072688). Confidence: medium.
- **Overleaf Lodge & Spa (Yachats, OR):** sells "Picnics by the Sea" as a lodge add-on. [Unique Inns](https://www.uniqueinns.com/specials/overleaf-lodge-and-spa/picnics-by-the-sea-8207). Confidence: medium.
- Staying power: Newport RI picnic businesses still going post-pandemic ([What's Up Newp](https://whatsupnewp.com/2022/05/pop-up-picnics-businesses-prove-they-have-staying-power/)); Smithsonian trend piece ([Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/innovation/picnics-are-back-180982102/)).

## 2. Micro-weddings / elopement packages
- **Simply Eloped:** founded by Janessa White (co-founder Matt Dalley); later bought by The Knot Worldwide. Elopements up to 15 guests, micro-weddings up to 50. [Simply Eloped team page](https://simplyeloped.com/team/), [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/simply-eloped). Confidence: high.
- **Pop-Up Weddings Calgary (2015, Erin Bjorkstedt):** three tiers, about CA$2,600 to $5,200. Rents one venue for a day and runs up to five couples back to back, splitting the venue cost. [Avenue Calgary](https://www.avenuecalgary.com/avenue-calgary-weddings/pop-up-weddings-calgary). Confidence: high.
- **Just Elope (Dallas, Jennifer Allen):** pitched micro-weddings to venues as a way to fill gaps in their calendars. [Black Enterprise](https://blackenterprise.com/black-woman-owned-micro-wedding-company-shares-5-tips-budget-friendly-wedding/). Confidence: medium.
- **PopWed Co. (DC):** an officiant and a photographer run quick weddings in public spaces. [Big Think](https://bigthink.com/?p=55660). Confidence: medium.
- **Evented (Edmonton):** pop-up elopement startup. [Edmonton Journal via PressReader](https://www.pressreader.com/canada/edmonton-journal/20170513/282595967835081). Confidence: medium.
- **Elope in Austin:** [Community Impact](https://communityimpact.com/austin/lake-travis-westlake/arts-entertainment/2022/04/18/local-wedding-business-elope-in-austin-redefines-weddings). Confidence: medium.
- Destination prices: Palm Springs all-inclusive $5,000 for up to 20 guests ([Visit Palm Springs PDF](https://assets.simpleviewinc.com/simpleview/image/upload/v1/clients/palmsprings/Micro_Wedding_Brochure_186cfd42-51a0-4fe4-82e7-6603edb1bd08.pdf)); Hilton Head from $5,000 ([Hilton Head](https://hiltonheadisland.org/offers/micro-wedding)). Confidence: high.

## 3. Backyard wedding / tent-plus-decor bundles
- Arizona "Backyard Wedding Bundle" $1,810 plus delivery: up to 10 farm tables, 400 ft of string lights, 16x16 dance floor, setup included. [My Event Rentals](https://reach.dog/shop/my-event-rentals/products/intimate-outdoor-wedding-package). Confidence: medium.
- MN/WI backyard-wedding planner sells tiered packages; tent rental billed separately; the top tier "reserves our entire decor inventory for one couple." [Squarespace packages page](https://celery-cornet-f4wb.squarespace.com/packages). Confidence: low-medium (vendor unclear).
- The pitch is skipping a $3,000-15,000 venue fee ([Tripleseat/EventUp](https://eventupblog.tripleseat.com/perfect-backyard-wedding)). Confidence: medium.

## 4. Mobile bars (horse trailers)
- **Patty Wagon (Tri-Cities WA):** two sisters converted a 40-year-old stock trailer; $600 for 4 hours. [Tri-Cities Area Journal of Business](https://www.tricitiesbusinessnews.com/2019/07/patty-wagon-touring-taps/). Confidence: high.
- **Wild Horse Mobile Bar:** started from a $400 horse trailer after a missed booking; five seasons in. [The Preferred List podcast](https://www.buzzsprout.com/2520237/episodes/18743572-episode-33-how-a-rusty-trailer-accidentally-became-a-mobile-bar-wild-horse-mobile-bar). Confidence: medium.
- **Sipping Southern (Williamsburg VA):** couple, 1984 horse trailer, launched while still working day jobs. [WYDaily](https://wydaily.com/brews-and-bites/2022/02/18/williamsburg-couple-turns-vintage-horse-trailer-into-mobile-bar-to-serve-parties-events/). Confidence: high.
- **Bars Without Boundaries (Saratoga Springs):** bar and cafe. [WNYT](https://wnyt.com/top-stories/wnyt-upstate-of-business/horse-trailer-turned-into-mobile-bar-and-cafe); Metro Detroit example ([Fox 2](https://www.fox2detroit.com/news/metro-detroit-woman-transforms-old-horse-trailer-into-mobile-bar-for-parties)). Confidence: high.
- Most of these bring the trailer and service but not the alcohol (the host buys it), which avoids liquor licensing.

## 5. Flower bars / carts
- **Bouquet Box Flower Bar:** turned a DIY bouquet concept into an experiential franchise, about $15k-50k to open. [Franchise Times, Apr 2026](https://www.franchisetimes.com/issue-archive/2026/april/bouquet-box-flower-bar-turns-diy-concept-into-an-experiential-franchise/article_af861320-508e-46d0-b98b-de71105f4e62.html). Confidence: high.
- **Flower Bar (Minneapolis, Andrea Corbin):** build-your-own studio with a portable version rented for showers and team events. [Mpls St Paul Magazine](https://mspmag.com/shop-and-style/build-your-own-bouquet-at-the-flower-bar). Confidence: high.
- **Elle Belle Bloom Bar (NM):** mother-daughter team pairing bloom bars with resin keepsakes. [Albuquerque Journal](https://www.abqjournal.com/business/local-floral-startup-blooms-under-mother-daughter-team/3038325). Confidence: high.
- Calluna Flower Truck ([Bethesda Chamber](https://web.greaterbethesdachamber.org/EVENT-PLANNING/Calluna-Flower-Truck-17203)); Little Bug Flower Bar from $100 ([Zola](https://www.zola.com/wedding-vendors/wedding-extras/little-bug-flower-bar)); The Bouquet Bar Chi from $150 ([Zola](https://www.zola.com/wedding-vendors/wedding-extras/the-bouquet-bar-chi)). Confidence: medium.

## 6. Tablescape rental boxes / subscriptions
- **Cloth + Gold (Bridget Rogers):** $17-24 per place setting; delivered the day before, picked up after, cleaning included. [Luxe](https://luxesource.com/architecture-design/cloth-gold-bridget-rogers/). Confidence: medium-high.
- **Tblscape:** $89/month subscription plus per-event rental. [AOL/Real Simple](https://www.aol.com/rented-entire-dinner-party-tablescape-210200484.html). Confidence: medium.
- **Social Studies (Texas):** ships a complete tabletop to the door. [PaperCity](https://www.papercitymag.com/home-design/social-studies-creative-tablescapes-rent-service-texas-women-entrepreneurs/). Confidence: high.
- Casamia boxes $50-95 for 4-8 people ([PaperCity](https://www.papercitymag.com/home-design/casamia-tablescapes-box-home-parties-hostess-coronavirus/)); Table + Teaspoon ([Domino](https://www.domino.com/content/table-teaspoon-rental-entertaining-tips/)); Lay London ([Livingetc](https://www.livingetc.com/whats-news/lay-london-table-laying-234780)). Confidence: medium.

## 7. 360 booths / selfie walls
- 360 booth rentals mostly run $600-1,500 per event; booths cost $4k-8k; part-timers do 2-4 events a month. Figures come from suppliers with an interest in the numbers. [Photo Booth Supply Co](https://blog.photoboothsupplyco.com/photo-booth-equipment-costs-roi-and-what-operators-actually-earn), [Check Cherry](https://www.checkcherry.com/articles/235-how-much-does-photo-booth-rental-c). Confidence: low-medium.
- A DFW turnkey 360 business listed at $15k revenue, $11k SDE: the commodity end. [BizQuest](https://images.bizquest.com/start-up-business/photobooth-and-360-booth-20-000-turnkey-business/BW2381776). Confidence: medium.
