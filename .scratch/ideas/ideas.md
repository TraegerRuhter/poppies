## How to read each line

**Title.** What it is. *Why it's worth it.*
`fit ●●● · effort S · payoff bookings · who her · src 02#8, B1`

- **fit:** ●●● suits a one-or-two-person coastal studio and the restrained
  brand; ●● workable with care; ● off-brand or risky (kept for range).
- **effort:** S = an afternoon, M = days or ongoing light work, L = a project.
- **payoff:** what it moves: *bookings*, *price* (higher price per job),
  *trust*, *reach*, *recurring* (repeat revenue), *off-season*.
- **who:** *dev* = the developer or Claude can do it now; *her* = needs the
  owner's input, skill or decision; *both*.
- **src:** where it came from. `01#3` = research stream 1, idea 3. `B12` =
  brainstorm idea 12. Sources for every factual claim are in those files.

**Tiers:** **Do now** (cheap, safe, unblocked) · **Next** (high value, needs a
decision or some build) · **Explore** (promising, needs testing or more
facts) · **Parked** (off-brand, premature or poor value, with the reason).

## If you read nothing else: the ten that matter most

**The headline from the local research:** no balloon *installation* studio is
based on the central coast. The one local balloon shop, Float Balloon Bar LC in
Lincoln City, looks like a gift-and-bouquet bar, and a listing showed it
"closed"; confirm in person. The nearest real installation rivals are 1.5–2.5
hours away. Salem Balloons and Flowers asks for two weeks' notice, and Magic
Balloons PDX does the same organic style and names the coast as a service area
(`research/08`). Lincoln County has
about 51,000 residents but around $600M a year in visitor spending, and
roughly half of visitors come from Portland and the Willamette Valley. Most
customers will book remotely, so a fast, visual, quote-friendly site matters
a lot. (`research/03` §1–§5)

1. **Become "the responsible balloon studio of the Oregon coast"** (#1–#6).
   Nothing is released, designs use air not helium, everything is collected,
   and she never claims "biodegradable". It's honest, the coast cares about it,
   few vendors say it out loud, and a 2026 helium shock makes air-filled work a
   real advantage.
2. **Publish "starting at" prices** (#21). No coastal vendor does. Couples
   rank price as their first filter, and "what drives the cost" is the
   most-searched pre-purchase topic. This is an open offering-model decision.
3. **Sell the occasion, not the balloons** (#24). The same arch sells for
   about $75 as "decorations" and $800–1,850 as a proposal package. Named,
   occasion-led packages are what the successful new businesses share.
4. **"The Arrival": vacation-rental surprise installs** (#30–#33), via coast
   property managers. Balloon studios already sell these for $250–550. The
   coast is a vacation-rental economy.
5. **"The Pavilion": the tent plus decor as a backyard venue** (#40–#44),
   with the fire permit handled. "Skip the $3,000–15,000 venue" is a proven
   pitch, and it settles tent ticket 07.
6. **A review engine from day one** (#60–#66). The review kit is drafted, and
   the Google profile checklist is ready.
7. **Host the coast's only wedding-pro gathering** (#80). No current wedding
   show runs on the Oregon coast, so Poppies could become the connector.
8. **Storefront and inn subscriptions** (#50–#53). Monthly installs at
   $175–300 are the only proven recurring balloon model, and coast towns are
   full of small shops and inns.
9. **Art as the brand engine** (#90–#95): commissions with title cards, a
   public piece each season, a gallery show. Prestige balloon artists are paid
   for exactly this.
10. **Answer the coast's questions in public** (#70–#79). Four explainers
    are already drafted in `drafts/`.

## Do now

Cheap, safe, and unblocked or nearly so.

### Trust and positioning

1. **Write the no-release pledge.** Nothing is ever released; this goes in the
   contract and on the site. *Oregon nearly banned releases in 2025 (HB 2592),
   so a new bill is likely; a seabird study found balloons the deadliest debris
   swallowed per item.*
   `fit ●●● · S · trust · her (confirm) · 06#1, 05#11, B41`
2. **Never say "biodegradable", "compostable" or "eco-friendly".** *A 2020/21
   study found "biodegradable" latex intact after 16 weeks; a major
   manufacturer admits no standard test exists; the FTC Green Guides make
   unqualified claims risky.*
   `fit ●●● · S · trust · both · 05§1.2, 06#6`
3. **Make "air, not helium" the signature.** Helium becomes a priced add-on.
   *It lasts longer and copes with damp better; helium supply was reportedly
   cut by about a third in 2026.*
   `fit ●●● · S · price · her · 06#2, 05#31, B18`
4. **Leave-no-trace teardown as a named standard:** a fragment sweep, plus a
   photo to the client. *Fixes the common "never collected" complaint, and
   suits beaches.*
   `fit ●●● · S · trust · her · 06#4, 06#20`
5. **Never use foil outdoors unsecured, or near power lines.**
   `fit ●●● · S · trust · her · 06#34`
6. **Put "contains natural latex" on quotes, and ask "any latex-allergic
   guests?" on the form.** *Latex allergy affects roughly 1–4% of people, and
   many hospitals ban latex balloons.*
   `fit ●●● · S · trust · dev (form) + her (latex-free offer) · 05#9, 06 rule 8`
7. **Reply within 24 hours, "no" included,** with referrals when she's booked.
   *Fixes the "ghosting" complaint.*
   `fit ●●● · S · trust · her · 06#19`

## Parked, with the reason

- **Marquee and mosaic numbers.** Commoditised (about $25 DIY frames) and
  party-supply in look. Only a custom serif version would fit. *(01#28)*
- **A permanent helium bar or retail counter.** Needs foot traffic and staff,
  and it's a gifting business. At most, a peak-season weekend pop-up in a
  partner's shop. *(01#29, B58)*
- **A paid Knot or WeddingWire listing in year one.** Poor reported lead
  quality, 12-month contracts, and balloons are rarely searched for first.
  Revisit at 7+ wedding reviews. *(04#35)*
- **Bark.** Expensive credits and heavy complaints. *(04 §4)*
- **A shipped national DIY kit.** "Hundreds" of kit shops now exist; only
  curation could set one apart, and it's a different business. *(01#11, 02#26)*
- **Arguing about prices in public videos.** Publish starting prices on the
  site instead; a balloon artist's pricing video drew a backlash. *(12#18)*
- **Licensing the format** to other towns. Premature until packages exist.
  *(01#27, 02#28)*
- **Teaching a business-starter course.** It trains her own competitors, and
  is premature. *(01#10)*
- **Sub-branded marketplace volume.** Splits the brand before it exists.
  *(01#30)*
- **Lobbying for Oregon's next release ban.** Credible, but political; revisit
  once she's established. *(06#27)*
- **ISO 20121 self-declaration.** Low value at this size. *(06#28)*
- **Fabric "forever balloons".** An interesting experiment, but outside the
  craft. *(06#30)*
- **Anything on a beach** until the state parks permit conditions are
  confirmed for the specific site. Décor must be freestanding, with no flames
  and a full sweep.
