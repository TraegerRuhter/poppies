# Twelve calls that are hers

Everything else in the plan can move without her. These can't. Each has a
recommendation and the reason, so she can disagree with the reasoning and not
just the answer. They replace the open checklist in
`../offering-model/issues/03-current-service-list.md` as the thing to put in
front of her; the same questions are on the plan page, where her answers are
saved.

| # | The call | Recommendation | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Do we keep the name if the POPPIE trademark is live?** | Check first. Keep "Poppies" only if a lawyer says it's safe | The same services, a near-identical word. A forced rename after signs and a domain costs far more than an hour's advice now |
| 2 | **Alone, or with a helper?** | Alone for year one, with a named helper on call for tent days | The tent needs two people. Everything else on the menu is sized for one |
| 3 | **Do we publish prices?** | Yes, as "from" prices | No coast rival does. It filters out the $75 enquiries before they cost her an evening |
| 4 | **Is $500 the minimum?** | Yes inside Lincoln County, with The Arrival, Collected and The Standing Order as the lower-priced doors | A coast job takes most of a day. Below $500 it pays less than $45 an hour |
| 5 | **Do we sell The Arrival through property managers?** | Yes. It's the first engine | Nobody on the coast offers it, it sells year-round, and the managers already reach every guest |
| 6 | **What is the tent?** | The Pavilion: always with decor, never rented bare | Bare rental competes with established firms on price. With decor it's a venue only she can offer, and it earns about $113 an hour |
| 7 | **Air only, nothing released, never "biodegradable"?** | Yes, as a written pledge | It's honest, the coast cares, and Oregon nearly banned releases in 2025 |
| 8 | **Sculpture: commissions, live twisting, or both?** | Commissions only. No live twisting, no licensed characters as advertised work | Twisting is a different trade at a different price, and it pulls the brand toward children's parties |
| 9 | **How many installs a week?** | A cap of three, with Sunday for teardowns | Three a week is the Strong scenario. Past that, quality and replies slip |
| 10 | **Where's home, and how far will she drive?** | Free from Lincoln City to Yachats; zones beyond, up to Tillamook and Florence | A zone reads better to a visitor than a per-mile charge, and it protects her day |
| 11 | **Which work do we turn down?** | Releases, bare tent rental, loud dealership work, anything under the minimum | Saying no in public is part of the position |
| 12 | **When do weddings start?** | Take them now if asked. Advertise them after the March styled shoot | A wedding page with no wedding photographs costs credibility |

## Four things only she knows

These aren't decisions. They're facts the plan had to guess.

1. **What has she actually made for paying customers**, and which piece is
   she fastest at? The fastest good piece should be the cheapest door.
2. **What would she rather never make again?**
3. **The tent:** where is it, what condition is it in, how long does it take
   two people to put up, and does the flame-resistance certificate exist?
4. **How many hours a week does she want this to be?** The careful scenario
   is about 14, the expected one 23, the strong one 35.
