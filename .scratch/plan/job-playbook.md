# The job playbook

How one job runs, from the enquiry to the review, and how the week fits
around it. Built from `../ideas/research/09` (operations), the review kit and
the pricing proposal. Her own way of working overrides anything here.

## One job, start to finish

| Step | What happens | When | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Reply** | A personal reply to every enquiry, "no" included. If booked, name someone else | Within 24 hours | 10 min |
| **2. Quote** | One page: the piece, the size, the colours, the price, teardown, what happens in wind. Run the two checks first | Within 48 hours | 20 min |
| **3. Hold the date** | Signed contract and half the price. The date isn't held without both | On acceptance | 10 min |
| **4. Design note** | A short message a week ahead: palette, placement, arrival time, access, latex allergies | 7 days before | 15 min |
| **5. Build** | Sections of 4–6 ft, made the day before or that morning, bagged, kept cool and out of the sun | Day before or day of | per the timed rate |
| **6. Install** | Arrive early. Finish an hour before guests | On the day | 1–2 h |
| **7. Photograph** | Before anyone arrives, in daylight, against the cleanest background. Then the same framing every time for the time-lapse | Before guests | 15 min |
| **8. Leave the card** | The review card, where the host will find it | On leaving | — |
| **9. Teardown** | Collect everything. Sweep for fragments. Send the client a photo of the clean space | Agreed window | 45 min |
| **10. Photos and the ask** | Send the best photos. The review link sits underneath, and the photos never depend on it | Within 2 days | 10 min |
| **11. Log it** | Hours, miles, price, where the enquiry came from | Same day | 5 min |

## The two checks on every quote

1. **The price is at least four times the materials plus mileage.**
2. **It pays $45 an hour or more,** counting the build, the install, the
   teardown and every minute of driving.

If a quote fails either, raise the price, add the teardown or travel fee, or
offer Collected instead.

## The week

Sized for one person and a cap of three installs.

| Day | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Monday** | The five numbers. Replies and quotes. Order stock |
| **Tuesday** | Route day: every shop on a Standing Order, in one loop |
| **Wednesday** | Outreach: two partner visits or calls. One piece of content |
| **Thursday** | Build day for the weekend |
| **Friday** | Installs. Collected pickups |
| **Saturday** | Installs |
| **Sunday** | Teardowns in the morning. Off after noon |

In June to September, Wednesday's outreach shrinks to one hour. In November
to March, Friday and Saturday often free up, and that time goes to the
explainers, case studies and the styled shoot.

## What goes in the car

- Sections, bagged, with the air conditioning on.
- Both hand pumps and a bag of spare balloons in every colour used.
- Frames, water bases and sandbags. More weight than seems necessary.
- Hooks, line, glue dots, scissors, a step stool.
- The review cards.
- A bin bag and a dustpan for the sweep.
- The insurance certificate, on the phone.

## Wind and weather

- Every outdoor piece is air-filled, weighted and tied at more than two
  points.
- The fallback is agreed at booking: a porch, a room, or the tent.
- She sets one wind speed above which the piece moves under cover, and
  states it on the quote. Start at 20 mph and adjust after the first
  outdoor season.
- No foil outdoors unless it's fixed down. Nothing near power lines.

## Words to keep ready

**The first reply.**
> Thank you for getting in touch. I'd love to hear more. For [date] in
> [town], a [piece] like the one you describe usually comes to about
> $[range], installed and taken down. If that suits, tell me your colours
> and send a photo of the space, and I'll send a proper quote by tomorrow.

**The no.**
> Thank you for thinking of us. I'm fully booked that weekend and wouldn't
> want to do it less than properly. [Name] does lovely work and may be free.
> I hope it's a wonderful day.

**Below the minimum.**
> For something that size, the best option is Collected: a ready-made
> garland for $136 that you pick up and hang yourself. Shall I hold one for
> Saturday?

**The property-manager pitch, in one breath.**
> When a guest tells you they're coming for a birthday or an anniversary,
> you can offer them The Arrival. We set up before check-in and collect
> after checkout, so your cleaners never touch it. It's $395 to the guest,
> and you keep 10%.

The photos message and the review reminder are in
`../ideas/drafts/review-kit.md`. The partner emails are in
`../ideas/drafts/partner-outreach.md`.

## The rules that don't bend

- Nothing is released, and nothing is left behind.
- No reward of any kind for a review.
- No job without a signed contract and a deposit.
- No tent without the fire permit, applied for 15 days ahead.
- "Contains natural latex" on every quote.
