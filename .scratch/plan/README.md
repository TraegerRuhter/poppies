# The plan

2026-11-11. One business plan for Poppies, built from everything else in
`.scratch/`. The research and the ideas list set out options; this folder
picks among them. **Every pick is a recommendation.** The owner's twelve
calls are in `decisions-for-her.md`.

It's also a page she can read on her phone, with the decisions as tap-to-
answer questions: see `page.html`, published as a private artifact (link in
`../HANDOFF.md`).

| File | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `the-plan.md` | **Start here.** The business in one sentence, the menu, three engines in order, the year, the money, the risks |
| `decisions-for-her.md` | Twelve calls only she can make, each with a recommendation and a reason |
| `menu.md` | Nine named pieces with customer wording, prices and terms. A proposal for offering-model ticket 04 |
| `first-90-days.md` | Week by week from 11 November to Valentine's weekend, with costs and a day-90 scoreboard |
| `year-one.md` | Month by month, three scenarios, what each piece earns, and checkpoints |
| `job-playbook.md` | One job from enquiry to review, the working week, and words to keep ready |
| `numbers.py` | The script behind every figure. Run `python3 numbers.py` |
| `page.html` | The plan as a page for the owner |

## What this rests on

- Prices and per-job hours: `../ideas/drafts/pricing-proposal.md` and
  `../ideas/model/`. One change: the arch is $550, because the model showed
  $450 pays under the $45 an hour target.
- The market and the rivals: `../ideas/research/03` and `08`.
- Setup costs and rules: `../ideas/drafts/startup-checklist.md` and
  `../ideas/research/10`. General information, not legal or tax advice.

All of that research rests on search snippets. Anything marked *check* in the
plan needs a phone call before money rides on it. The scenarios are examples,
not forecasts.
