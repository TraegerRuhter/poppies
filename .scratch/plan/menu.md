# The menu: nine pieces

A proposed service menu, written the way a customer would read it. It fills
offering-model ticket 04 as a **proposal**; prices come from
`../ideas/drafts/pricing-proposal.md`. Nothing here goes on the site until
she's agreed it (website ticket 05).

**Names.** The pieces are named for the coast. None of the names has been
checked against trademarks; do that with the POPPIE check. Avoid the phrase
"a pop of colour", which is a filed mark.

**The house rules, said on every page:** built with air, not helium. Nothing
is ever released. Everything is collected. Contains natural latex.

---

## For celebrations

### The Tideline
*An organic garland, built for your space.*

A loose, layered line of balloons in your colours: along a mantel, up a stair
rail, around a doorway, over the cake table. Designed for the room and
installed before your guests arrive.

- **$28 a foot.** Most are 12 to 25 feet.
- Lush or chrome finish: $35 a foot. With dried florals or foils: from $42.
- **From $500**, installed, anywhere from Lincoln City to Yachats.

### The Headland
*A freestanding arch.*

An 8 to 10 foot organic arch that stands on its own: for the entrance, the
photo spot or the ceremony. Weighted for coast wind.

- **$550**, installed.

### The Arrival
*The celebration is already there when they open the door.*

For a birthday, an anniversary or a reunion in a rental house. We set up
before check-in: a garland, a number or name piece, and a handwritten card.
You send the door code; we send a photo when it's ready. We come back and
collect everything after checkout.

- **$395**, collection included.
- Seven days' notice. Three days for a small rush fee.
- Often bought as a gift by someone who can't be there.

### The Question
*A proposal setup.*

One sculptural piece at your rental, on private land or at a viewpoint that
allows it. In place an hour before, gone an hour after. We can bring a
photographer who knows to stay out of sight.

- **From $750.** From $1,200 with a photographer.

### Collected
*A ready-made garland to take with you.*

An 8 foot garland in one of our palettes, made that morning. Pick it up on
Friday or Saturday and hang it yourself with the hooks we include.

- **$136.** No minimum, no install.

## For weddings and larger gatherings

### The Cloud
*A ceiling installation.*

Balloons overhead: above a dance floor, a long table or the length of a tent.
Priced by the area and the height.

- **From $450** for a small room. **From $1,200** for a statement piece.
- In the tent, priced per bay.

### The Pavilion
*A venue that comes to you.*

Our 20×40 foot tent on your lawn, a rental house's garden or private land,
dressed with a ceiling Cloud and a Tideline at the entrance. We handle the
fire permit and bring the insurance certificate. Room for about 60 seated.

- **From $3,000**, tent, decor, permit and teardown included.
- Booked at least 30 days ahead, because the permit needs 15.
- Stay a second day for brunch and we restyle it at a discount.

*Before this is offered: find the tent's flame-resistance certificate,
confirm insurance covers it, and time one full setup. See
`decisions-for-her.md`, call 6.*

## For art

### The Commission
*A sculpture, made once.*

A figure, a creature or a form, designed and built for you: an octopus for
an aquarium evening, a heron for a gallery opening, a company's mascot for an
anniversary. Each comes with a title card.

- **From $250.** Priced by the hours it takes, at $60 to $75 an hour, plus
  materials. We quote before we start.
- No licensed characters.

## For coast businesses

### The Standing Order
*A fresh piece in your window every month.*

A 10 foot installation for a shop, gallery, inn or tasting room, changed
monthly with the season and the coast's calendar: spring break, the kite
festivals, the holiday strolls.

- **$240 a month.** Three-month minimum. Skip a month with a week's notice.
- Holiday lobbies and one-off openings are quoted separately; a
  ribbon-cutting garland with an oversized ribbon is **$350**.

## Terms, in plain words

| Term | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Minimum** | $500 in Lincoln County. $650 for Tillamook or Florence. $800 inland. The Arrival, Collected and The Standing Order have their own prices |
| **Travel** | Free from Lincoln City to Yachats. A flat fee per zone beyond |
| **Teardown** | $120 local, when we come back to take it down. Included in The Arrival and The Pavilion |
| **Booking** | A week's notice as standard. Inside a week, a 15% rush fee if we can do it |
| **Deposit** | Half to hold the date. It moves to a new date once, with 30 days' notice |
| **Helium** | On request, $3 a balloon. Our work is designed not to need it |
| **Weather** | Outdoors, we build for wind. Above a set wind speed we move it indoors or under cover, and we agree the fallback when you book |
| **Longevity** | Indoors, air-filled work looks its best for several days and lasts longer. Outdoors, plan on the day |

## What's deliberately not on the menu

- **Balloon releases.** Never. We offer bubbles, ribbon wands or a planted
  tree instead.
- **Bare tent rental.** The coast's rental firms do that well, and we'd
  rather send them work.
- **Live balloon twisting at parties.** A different trade with different
  pricing (*her call*: see `decisions-for-her.md`, call 8).
- **Licensed characters** as advertised work.
- **Anything called "biodegradable" or "eco-friendly".** The claims don't
  hold up, so we don't make them.

## The rule for adding a piece

A new piece joins the menu when it has been made three times for paying
customers, it pays $45 an hour or more with the drive counted, and there's a
photograph good enough for the site. Until then it's a custom quote.
