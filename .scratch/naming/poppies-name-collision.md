# Poppies — name collision research

Status: needs-info
Date: 2026-08-12

**Verdict: worth caution, not a hard stop — no Oregon balloon business called Poppies was found, but two established Oregon event vendors already trade under the exact word "Poppies" (one of them an event-rental company that explicitly serves the Oregon coast), and a Los Angeles wedding-and-events studio already owns `poppiesevents.com` with poppy-flower branding, which is one character away from `poppies.events`.**

Important caveat on coverage, stated up front: **the two authoritative registries — Oregon Secretary of State and USPTO — were unreachable from this session.** Both domains are blocked by the network egress proxy, as are OpenCorporates and Bizapedia. Everything below comes from web-search results and their snippets, not from fetched pages or registry queries. See "What I could not determine" — that section is not boilerplate, it is load-bearing here.

## HIGH RISK — Oregon event vendors already using "Poppies"

### Poppies & Top Hats — Junction City / Eugene, OR (specialty event rentals)

The single most relevant Oregon finding. An event-rental company trading under the exact word "Poppies," in the Eugene corridor, that serves the Oregon coast.

- Site: https://www.poppiesandtophats.com/ — titled "Home | Eugene, Oregon | Poppies & Top Hats Specialty Event Rentals"
- The Knot listing: https://www.theknot.com/marketplace/poppies-and-top-hats-junction-city-or-2036605
- Chamber of Commerce directory: https://www.chamberofcommerce.com/business-directory/oregon/junction-city/wedding-service/2026333162-poppies-and-top-hats-specialty-event-rentals

What the sources say: a wedding and special-event rental company based in Junction City, Oregon, owned by Lorna Kennedy, started in 2015. Serves Eugene, Springfield and Lane County, providing rentals for weddings, anniversaries, birthdays, bridal and baby showers, company events, and Bar/Bat Mitzvahs. One search summary (from the WeddingWire Eugene rentals directory, https://www.weddingwire.com/c/or-oregon/eugene-bend-medford/wedding-event-rentals/801-12-rca.html) described the service area as "S. Oregon, N. California, and the Oregon Coast."

Why it collides: same distinctive word, same industry (party/event decor and rentals), same state, overlapping service territory including the coast. This is the closest thing to a genuine competitor found. The name is not identical — "Poppies & Top Hats" vs "Poppies" — but a bride searching "Poppies event Oregon" will hit both, and this business has a ~11-year head start on common-law rights in the Oregon event market.

Note the discrepancy: the site title says Eugene, The Knot says Junction City. Both may be true (Junction City is ~15 miles from Eugene); I did not resolve it.

## MEDIUM RISK — the florist confusion problem is real and already occupied

The brief predicted that a poppy-flower logo on a business named "Poppies" would read as a florist. That prediction is confirmed: in Oregon, the "Poppies" name is *already* predominantly a florist name. Every Oregon business found using the plural "Poppies" is a flower business. In addition to the two florist entries above:

- **Wild Poppy Florist — Bend, OR.** 56825 Venture Ln #108, Bend, (541) 593-2027. https://www.bendoregonflorist.com/ · https://www.yelp.com/biz/wild-poppy-florist-bend · https://www.facebook.com/wildpoppyflorists/ — singular, geographically distant (Central Oregon, not the coast), lower concern on its own but adds to the crowding.

Practical consequence: a poppy-flower logo plus the name "Poppies" will place her, in the mind of an Oregon customer and in local search results, inside a category already held by three or four flower businesses. That is a marketing/discoverability cost more than a legal one, but it is the cost most likely to be felt day to day.

## Domain: poppies.events

- DNS queries to 8.8.8.8 for `poppies.events` returned **NXDOMAIN** for NS, A and SOA — identical to a control string I made up (`thisnameisdefinitelynotregistered12345.events`), and unlike `poppiesevents.com` and `poppiesandtophats.com`, which both answered normally. That is consistent with the domain being unregistered.
- This is **not proof of availability**. A registered domain with no nameservers delegated returns NXDOMAIN too. I could not reach any registrar or WHOIS service to confirm (no `whois` binary in this environment, and HTTPS to registrar/WHOIS sites is blocked). Check availability at a registrar before relying on it.

## What I could not determine

Stated plainly, because several of these are the questions that actually matter:

1. **The Oregon Secretary of State business registry was never searched.** `sos.oregon.gov` and `egov.sos.state.or.us` are both blocked by this session's egress proxy (the proxy logged a 403 on CONNECT to `egov.sos.state.or.us:443`). **I do not know whether "Poppies" is currently registered as an Oregon business name or assumed business name (ABN).** Nothing in this document should be read as saying the registry is clear — the registry was not consulted. This is the single most important open item, and it is a free five-minute check at https://sos.oregon.gov/business/pages/find.aspx.
2. **No USPTO search was performed.** `tmsearch.uspto.gov` is blocked. Search-engine results surfaced no federal mark for the bare word "POPPIES," and no POPPY-formative mark in Class 41 covering party or event planning — but that is search-engine coverage of Justia/Trademarkia pages, not a TESS query, and it cannot be relied on. The nearest thing found was **PINE & POPPY** (Serial 90523031, https://trademarks.justia.com/905/23/pine-90523031.html), described in a search summary as covering rental of furniture and lighting for decorating parties, weddings and special events — a different mark, but evidence that poppy-formative marks are being filed in the event-rental space. **Verify at https://tmsearch.uspto.gov before registering.**
3. **The Oregon state trademark registry** (separate from the federal one, administered by the Secretary of State) was not checked at all — same egress block.
4. **No page was fetched directly.** Every finding above rests on web-search result titles and snippet summaries. Names, addresses, owner names, founding dates and service areas are reported as the sources stated them; I could not open the pages to verify. Treat specifics (the Golden CO vs Birmingham AL discrepancy for Poppy Balloon Co., the Eugene vs Junction City discrepancy for Poppies & Top Hats) as unconfirmed.
5. **Google Business / Maps listings were not queried directly**, nor was Instagram or Facebook search. Coverage of those platforms is limited to what the web search surfaced.
6. **Absence of evidence on the coast.** Searches for a "Poppies" or "Poppy" balloon, party, or event business in Astoria, Cannon Beach, Seaside, Tillamook, Lincoln City, Newport, Florence, Coos Bay or Bandon returned nothing. That is a null result from general web search, not a clean bill of health — a brand-new coastal balloon artist with an Instagram-only presence would very plausibly not surface this way. It should not be read as "the coast is clear."
