# Choose the pricing basis

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 01, 04

## Question

On what basis is a Poppies job priced — per linear foot, per piece, by package,
by day rate, or quote-only every time?

The decision has to survive three awkward cases:

- **The same garland at a wedding and a birthday.** Is it the same price? If not,
  what is the honest justification, and does it survive a customer asking?
- **A coastal venue two hours away.** Where does travel stop being absorbed and
  start being billed, and is that a line on the quote or baked into a minimum?
- **The sculptural work.** A caterpillar has no linear footage. Does figure work
  get its own basis, and does that make the menu incoherent?

Also settle whether a **booking minimum** exists, and whether a **"starting at"
anchor** appears publicly — which determines whether the website shows any numbers
at all, currently sitting in the map's fog.

Ground the recommendation in the benchmarks from ticket 01. Where the
recommendation departs from what the market does, say so explicitly and why.

## Why this blocks

Packaging shape depends on it: tiers are only meaningful if there is a basis for
what separates one tier from the next.
