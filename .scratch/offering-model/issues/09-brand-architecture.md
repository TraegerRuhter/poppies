# Decide whether Poppies stays one brand

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 08

## Question

As offerings grow, does Poppies remain a single flat brand, or become a parent
with named sub-brands?

Flat is almost always right at this size, so the burden of proof sits on
splitting. The question is whether anything in the growth sequence forces it —
the tent as a venue play is the obvious candidate, since a venue and a balloon
studio are not naturally the same brand.

Settle the trading-name form at the same time, since it is the same decision
wearing a different hat: whether the business is "Poppies," "Poppies Balloon Co.,"
or something with a qualifier that distinguishes it from the three other Oregon
"Poppies" already working weddings. The name-collision research recommended a
qualifier; this ticket decides whether the offering model agrees.

Note the constraint this is under: the trading name is **a single config value**
on the website, deliberately, so this decision stays cheap right up until the
domain is bought.

## Why this blocks

Nothing — it is the last ticket. Reaching it means the way is clear.
