# Draft the service menu as a concrete artifact

Type: prototype
Status: open
Blocked by: 02, 03

## Question

What does the actual menu look like, written out?

Not a taxonomy discussion — a real document, in her voice, that a customer could
read: the named offerings, what each includes, what each excludes, and where the
boundaries between them sit. Rough and reactable beats complete and abstract.

The point is to raise the fidelity of the argument. "À la carte versus packages"
is unresolvable in the abstract and obvious the moment two drafts sit side by
side, so **produce more than one shape** — at minimum a flat à la carte list and a
tiered version of the same offerings — and let the comparison do the work.

Carry forward what ticket 02 found about how comparable businesses structure
menus, and what ticket 03 learned about what she actually makes.

Mark every item the draft invented rather than observed. Those become questions
for her.

## Why this blocks

The pricing basis and the packaging shape are both easier to judge against a
concrete menu than in the abstract, so this sits upstream of both.
