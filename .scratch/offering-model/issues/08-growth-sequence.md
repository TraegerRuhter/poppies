# Order the growth sequence and its preconditions

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 06, 07

## Question

What is offering number two, what is number three, and what has to be true before
each one can exist?

The preconditions matter more than the order. For each step, name what gates it —
portfolio evidence, equipment, insurance, a second pair of hands, or simply
demand that has not appeared yet.

Weddings are the assumed next step, and the assumption deserves pressure:

- The site deliberately does **not** pitch weddings yet, because there is no
  wedding portfolio. What is the cheapest honest route to that first wedding job
   — a styled shoot, a friend's wedding at cost, a partnership with a coast
  planner or florist?
- Three of the four Oregon "Poppies" businesses already work weddings. Is entering
  that market as the fourth actually the best next step, or is it simply the most
  obvious one?
- What does the *corporate* and *civic* market look like by comparison — grand
  openings, holiday installations, storefronts? Lower glamour, but repeat
  customers, weekday work, no emotional stakes, and nobody named Poppies in it.

## Why this blocks

It is the last substantive input to the proposal, and it determines what the
website will need to say a year from now.
