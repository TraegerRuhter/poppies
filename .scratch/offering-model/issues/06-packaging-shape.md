# Choose the packaging shape and the rule for adding an offering

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 04, 05

## Question

How are the offerings grouped and presented — and what is the rule that says
where a *new* offering goes when it arrives?

The second half is the one the destination actually turns on. A service list is
easy; what has been missing is the shape that lets a fourth and fifth offering
arrive without the menu, the brand, or the website needing a rethink.

Decide:

- **À la carte, tiered packages, or a hybrid** — a small set of packages with
  add-ons is the obvious hybrid, but it has to be judged against the drafts from
  ticket 04, not asserted.
- **What dimension the grouping runs along** — occasion (birthday, wedding, baby
  shower), scale (petite, standard, grand), or component (garland, arch,
  sculpture). Each implies a different answer for where a new offering slots in,
  and only one of them can be primary.
- **The admission rule.** Given a new thing she wants to sell, what test decides
  whether it is a new package, a new add-on, or a new category? Write it as a rule
  someone else could apply.

## Why this blocks

The website's content model is a direct encoding of this. The grouping dimension
chosen here determines whether the two doorway paths survive as an offering split
or remain purely a matter of register — one of the open patches in the map's fog.
