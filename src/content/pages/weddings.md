## What we can make for your day

- **A ceremony arch**, or a pair of columns, in whites, creams and muted tones.
- **Garlands** for the head table, the bar or the cake.
- **An entrance** that tells guests they have arrived.

Everything is designed in your palette and built on site before guests arrive. If you are planning a wedding, tell us early — we will talk through the venue, the light, and what holds up outdoors on the coast.
