## What we make

- **Garlands** along a table, a mantel, or the wall behind the cake.
- **Arches and backdrops** for the photo everyone ends up taking.
- **Sculpture** — one-off figures, designed with you.

Every piece is designed around your colours and built on site, so nobody is wrestling with balloons on the morning of the party.
